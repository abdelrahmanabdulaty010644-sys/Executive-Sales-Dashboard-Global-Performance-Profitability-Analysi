# 📊 Executive Sales Dashboard: Global Performance & Profitability Analysis

## 🎯 Project Overview
This Power BI project delivers a high-level **Executive Sales Dashboard** designed to track and analyze global sales performance across multiple countries, product categories, and fiscal years. The goal was to transform raw sales transactions into interactive strategic insights, highlighting top-performing regions and highly profitable product lines to assist C-level executives in data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Key Business Insights

### 💰 Core Financial KPIs
* **Total Sales:** `$110.34M`
* **Total Profit:** `$12.55M`
* **Profit Margin:** `11%` (0.11)

### 🌍 Geographical Market Performance
* **United States:** Dominates the global market with **$63.31M** in sales.
* **Canada:** `$16.44M`
* **Australia:** `$10.68M`
* **United Kingdom:** `$7.70M`
* **France:** `$7.29M`
* **Top Regional Specifics:** The **Southwest** region emerged as the absolute top sales engine, generating **$24.32M**.

### 📅 Macro Sales Trends Over Time
* **2017:** $11M  
* **2018:** $26M  
* **2019:** $36M *(Peak Performance Year)*  
* **2020:** $22M  

### 📦 Product Breakdown
* **Highest Profit Category:** **Bikes** outperformed all other sectors, single-handedly generating **$10.52M** of the total profit.

## 🛠️ Power BI Implementation & Techniques
* **Advanced DAX Formulas:** Authored custom DAX measures to dynamic calculate precise business KPIs including Total Sales, Net Profit, and Profit Margin.
* **Dynamic Staging & Filtering:** Implemented synchronized multi-attribute **Slicers** allowing executives to slice data instantly by Country, Category, and Year.
* **Comprehensive Visualization Stack:** Designed a balanced visual hierarchy using **Gauge Charts** (for margin tracking), **Donut & Pie Charts** (for categorical splits), **Line Charts** (for historical trends), and **Bar Charts** (for regional comparisons).
